# ASCENDIA NON-NEGOTIABLES

## Design

- Every section must have a purpose.
- Every pixel should earn its place.
- Simplicity over complexity.
- Whitespace is a design element.
- Never copy another website directly.

---

## User Experience

- Users should understand our value within 5 seconds.
- Navigation must always feel obvious.
- Every interaction should feel intentional.
- Mobile experience is equal to desktop.
- Accessibility is mandatory.

---

## Motion

- Motion should tell a story.
- Animations should never exist just to look cool.
- Every transition should have a purpose.
- Respect users who prefer reduced motion.

---

## Performance

- Lighthouse target above 95.
- Lazy load where appropriate.
- Optimize images before adding them.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Performance is part of the product.

---

## Content

- No lorem ipsum.
- No fake testimonials.
- No fake client logos.
- No exaggerated claims.
- Clear, confident and honest copy.

---

## Engineering

- Reusable components first.
- No duplicate code.
- Server Components by default.
- Strong TypeScript.
- Clean folder structure.

---

## Brand

Ascendia should always feel:

- Premium
- Calm
- Confident
- Strategic
- Modern
- Memorable

Ascendia should never feel:

- Cheap
- Generic
- Over-animated
- Template-based
- Cluttered
